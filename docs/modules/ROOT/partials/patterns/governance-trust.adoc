=== Governance trust and trust registries

_Governance trust_ reflects confidence in how a credential was issued and managed.
It is distinct from technical trust (whether the signature is cryptographically valid) and from reputation trust (whether the named actor is personally trustworthy).

When a CAWG identity assertion includes a reference to a https://cawg.io/identity/1.3/#_trust_registry_requirements[trust registry], the named actor is claiming recognition by a governing authority operating under a published governance framework.
This is an additional trust signal beyond the cryptographic signature itself.

==== Displaying governance trust

When a trust registry entry is present in an identity assertion, implementations should:

* Identify the governing authority by name and link to the governance framework or trust registry where available.
* Clearly distinguish the governance trust signal from the cryptographic verification signal — these represent different kinds of assurance.
* Apply a visual hierarchy that places governance trust signals at an appropriate level of prominence (typically L2 or L3 in progressive disclosure).

When no trust registry entry is present, the identity assertion may still be technically valid and well-formed.
Implementations must not treat the absence of a trust registry entry as a failure or warning state.

==== Displaying trust evaluation outcomes

An identity assertion may be evaluated against a trust registry and yield one of three outcomes as defined by the https://cawg.io/identity/1.3/[CAWG identity assertion specification]:

Trusted:: The named actor was found in the trust registry and the credential was not revoked at the time of signing.
Well-formed:: The identity assertion is cryptographically valid but no applicable trust registry entry was found.
Revoked:: The credential used to sign the identity assertion was revoked at the time of signing.

These outcomes should be communicated to users in plain language.
The "Trusted" and "Well-formed" outcomes describe the level of assurance established by the credential chain — not an endorsement of the named actor's character or the truthfulness of the content.

NOTE: Trust registry support is introduced in version 1.3 of the CAWG identity assertion specification. Implementations targeting earlier versions of the specification will not have a trust registry field to display.
